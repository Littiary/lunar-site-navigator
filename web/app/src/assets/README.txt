Renders of the Unreal planner used on the landing page (originals and what each shows: docs/unreal/pitch/README.txt).

b_product_two_sites.jpg, c_key_moment_near_ridge.jpg, e_lander_lit_side.jpg
    The PNGs of the same names scaled to 1600 px wide, JPEG quality 84.

hero_loop_720p.mp4, hero_loop_poster.jpg
    Cut from f_pitch_video_1080p60.mp4: 23.95 to 27.85 s (wide view of Shackleton crater rim crest with lander,
    mast and boulders while 36 h pass from 2027-09-19T13:00:00Z) followed by 0 to 5.85 s (camera move from high
    above down to the lander at the same site and time; a camera move only, no descent is simulated). Cropped to 1776 x 999 from the
    top left (x 72) so that the caption line at the bottom is left out, scaled to 1280 x 720, 30 frames per second,
    H.264 (CRF 22), no audio. The poster is its first frame. Lander, mast and boulders are scale objects of the
    team's own design, not part of any package.

key_moment_ridge_720p.mp4, key_moment_ridge_poster.jpg
    c_key_moment_near_ridge.mp4 scaled to 1280 x 720, 30 frames per second, H.264 (CRF 23), no audio; the poster
    is the frame at 7.5 s.

Encoded with FFmpeg 9.0.2 (the build that the Python package imageio-ffmpeg ships).
